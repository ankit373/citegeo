Before you expose this:

  CiteGEO has no authentication. Anyone who reaches the port can read every
  stored project, run and raw model answer, and can spend your provider
  credit. Put an authenticating proxy in front of it, and consider
  networkPolicy.enabled=true with an explicit ingressFrom.

{{- if not .Values.persistence.enabled }}

  persistence.enabled is false, so projects, runs and evidence live in an
  emptyDir and are lost when the pod restarts.
{{- end }}
{{- if and (not .Values.secrets.existingSecret) (not .Values.secrets.create) }}
