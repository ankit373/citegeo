# Raising your standing, one question at a time

The procedure to follow between runs. It assumes a project with tracked
questions and at least one completed run.

## The loop

1. Open **Scores** and read **What would move this**. Work the moves tagged
   *Raises the score* first. The other two tags do not move it, and doing them
   first is the usual way a quarter disappears.
2. Open **Questions you never appear in** and click one. The panel leads with
   what the models credited the winners with, in their own words.
3. Read every quote for the top three names. Each one states a criterion:
   what the product is credited with, for whom, and against what limit.
4. Write down the criteria that repeat across models. A claim two assistants
   make about the same product is the association you are competing with.
5. Decide, honestly, whether you meet the criterion. If you do not, this is a
   product decision, not a content one, and no amount of publishing fixes it.
6. If you do meet it, the gap is that nothing the models read says so in those
   words. Put it on a page in that shape: the claim, who it is for, the limit
   it holds under, and the evidence.
7. Re-run the set. **Movement** on Scores shows the change, one point per run.

## Reading the panel

- **Verdict** says which state the question is in. "No answer here named you,
  though N assistants name you on other questions" means the gap is the
  question, not the brand, and is the cheapest kind to close.
- **Named you / did not** names the assistants individually. One assistant
  missing you is a gap in what that assistant read.
- **What the models credited** is the standard the question is answered
  against. A model repeats what its sources say about a product.
- **What they said about you** carries the unkind lines too. A negative
  mention is worth acting on before an absence, because it is already in the
  answer and only the judgement needs changing.
- **What these answers read** lists the sources. When it is empty, no model
  that ran can search the web, so nothing here points at a page. Add a
  provider with web search before treating the source list as a finding.

## What does not work

- Adding questions. It widens what is measured. A new question is a new place
  to be absent from, and the score falls rather than rises.
- Naming yourself in a question. The model will discuss whatever the question
  names, so the appearance is not earned and the tool excludes it from
  visibility for that reason.
- One run. A model's answer varies between runs. Two points make a line; one
  makes a number that will move on its own.

## Cadence

Run the set on a schedule rather than by hand, and leave at least a week
between a published change and the run you judge it by. Anything a model
learns about a page, it learns from a source it re-reads on its own timetable.
